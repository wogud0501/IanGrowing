The interface uses an almost entirely achromatic palette: black, near-black, charcoal, gray, light gray, off-white and white.

No colorful mobile-game interface.

The interface should feel cold, urban, slightly bureaucratic and subtly humorous, supporting the game's understated black comedy about work, debt, education, money and adulthood.

Use contrast, typography, inversion, line weight, patterns, iconography and animation instead of bright colors to communicate hierarchy and state.

Selected elements may invert from black to white.

Important numbers should be large and highly readable.

Secondary information should use muted gray.

The UI must support Korean text with excellent readability.

Cohesive with a detailed 2.5D pixel-art open world.
