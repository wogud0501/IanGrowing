# Official design excerpts used in this audit

Read on 2026-11-06 through Firecrawl from [the user's official design](https://chatgpt.com/share/6ae9f8e1-a144-83ee-99f0-06048d06d9c5). Current user instructions take precedence where that long conversation proposes alternatives (e.g. 90 missions). The existing 60 main missions remain authoritative for this implementation.

The original job table lists convenience employee 1,000G, office employee 3,000G, developer 7,000G, and investment analyst 10,000G. CEO has variable business income; king of unemployment has 0G and a happiness concept. The latter two are future/special unlocks, not normal paid jobs. The source also describes an 8-hour offline cap. Phase 2.4 cycle timings and battle balance are implementation data, not figures claimed to come from that table.
